LangBot Enterprise Support is for organizations that need an **official support relationship** and **formal procurement documents**. Your subscription also directly funds continued LangBot development.

<Info>
LangBot remains free under Apache License 2.0. Enterprise Support is **not a commercial-use license**: it unlocks no features and does not restrict organizations that don't subscribe.
</Info>
